# /weekly-review

A standing review to keep the workshop alive (not a graveyard).

## Steps

1. **Stale projects:** scan `leads/`, `clients/`, `projects/` for `STATUS.md`
   files not touched recently; list each with its last date and next action.
2. **This week's decisions:** list ADRs in `decisions/` dated this week; one-line
   each.
3. **Missed promotions:** flag any lead that's clearly active but still in
   `leads/`, or any finished client not yet archived.
4. **Retirement:** suggest anything worth deleting or archiving — be willing to
   throw tools out.
5. Output a short punch list. File nothing automatically; let the operator decide.
